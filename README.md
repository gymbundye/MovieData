# My Data Analysis CAPSTONE PROJECT

## <h1 style="text-align:center;">MOVIE DATA</h1>

**MovieData** is an extension of a real-life activity that my friends and I do called **Movie Club**. Every week we pick a movie, watch the movie, and then talk about the movie. We live very far from each other, so we don't watch them together, but all movies must be watched by 9:00 PM on Tuesday.

We have a few other rules that we follow:
- Movies cannot be longer than 2 hours and 15 minutes.
- Movies have to be in English; we love foreign films, but sometimes we have other responsibilities and cannot read a whole movie.
- If it is the month of October, you have to pick a horror film.
- If it is the month of December, you have to pick a Christmas movie.
- If it is the month of your birthday, then you get to pick a bonus movie that can be any length of time, and the Club has the whole month to watch it.
- Even if you have seen the movie before you have to watch it during that week time period.
- You may never pick the all-girl Ghostbusters remake.
  

Easy enough, right?

### What Questions Does MovieData Aim to Answer?

Since we have been doing the club, we have been keeping a Google Sheet that we share to put our rankings and other data. I thought it would be a great idea to clean and analyze that data. Then we can see:

- Who has picked the best-ranked movies?
- Who has picked the most comedies?
- Who has picked the longest movie?
- etc.

It will be a fun thing to show my buddies, and hopefully, you have some fun as well.
