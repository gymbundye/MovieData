                                            <h1>MOVIE DATA<h1>     

MovieData is an extension of a real life activity that my friends and I do called Movie Club. Every week we pick a movie,
watch the movie, and then talk about the movie. We live very far from each other so we dont watch them together but all movies
must be watched by 9:00pm on tuesday. We have a few other rules that we follow. Movies can not be longer 2hrs and 15mins. Movies have 
to be in english, we love foreign films but sometimes we have other reponsibilities and can not read a whole movie. If it is the 
month of October you have to pick a horror film. If it is the month of December you have to pick a Christmas Movie. If it is the month
of your Birthday then you get to pick a bonus movie that can be any length of time and the Club has the Whole month to watch it. You may 
never pick the all girl Ghostbusters remake. Easy enough, right?

What questions do Movie Data Aim to answer? Since we have been doing the club we have been keeping a google sheet that we share to put
our rankings and other data. I thought it we be a great idea to clean and analyze that data. Then we can see who has picked the best ranked movies,
who has picked the most comedies, who has picked the longest movie, etc. It will be a fun thing to show my buddies and hopefully you have some 
fun as well.
